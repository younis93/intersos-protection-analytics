# Subject: Request for Your Endorsement and HQ Approval - Iraq Legal Platform Pilot

Dear [Head of Mission's name],

I am writing to request your endorsement for a controlled pilot of the **Iraq Legal Platform** with designated Iraq Mission managers, officers, and team leaders. Subject to your agreement, I kindly request your support to submit the attached proposal to HQ for review and approval by the relevant ICT, Protection, Information Management, and Data Protection focal points.

The Legal Platform is a locally run application designed to make legal-programme data review, analysis, and reporting more accessible to non-technical users. It enables authorized staff to load the Legal Platform CSV files that they have already downloaded from Knack under their existing access permissions. The application supports structured review of data quality, service delivery, workload, trends, and indicators without requiring coding or advanced analytical skills.

The proposed pilot would help the Mission to:

- identify possible data-quality issues, such as duplicate records, incomplete fields, inconsistent relationships between assessments and services, and invalid dates or contact details;
- review legal-service delivery, case follow-up, detention and deportation data through clear tables, filters, and visual summaries;
- identify inconsistent detention records for staff correction, then present the validated and filtered detention data through multiple charts for clearer operational review;
- monitor workload and key programme indicators more consistently;
- prepare verified reporting outputs and exports more efficiently; and
- give managers and team leaders a clearer basis for follow-up with relevant staff.

For clarity, the application is **not an AI system for protection analysis or decision-making**. It does not assess, rank, prioritize, refer, determine eligibility for, or make recommendations about individual cases or people. Its findings are review flags and analytical summaries only. Every finding, indicator, export, and report remains subject to staff verification against the source data, and the authorized staff member remains fully accountable for its use and any resulting decision.

## Application pages and purpose

| Page | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Provides a high-level view of key Legal Platform data, activity trends, service delivery, and operational performance. |
| Indicator Reporting | Calculates filterable programme indicators, provides trend analysis, allows review of underlying beneficiary IDs, and exports Excel and narrative reports. |
| Beneficiaries Review | Identifies possible duplicate, contact, age, and case-readiness issues for staff review. |
| Assessments Review | Checks assessment continuity, service coverage, and detention-related data consistency. |
| Legal Services Review | Highlights possible duplicate services, missing documents, and broken assessment-service links. |
| Awareness Review | Supports review of possible duplicate participants and invalid contact information. |
| Detention | Provides filterable charts and records on detention circumstances, locations, authorities, charges, and case status, and supports reconciliation of inconsistent records for staff review. |
| Deportation | Provides a filterable dashboard of deportation records, including KPIs, time trends, breakdown charts, searchable records, and Excel export. |
| Lawyer Overview | Summarizes workload, service delivery, and case complexity across legal teams. |
| Analytics Studio | Provides ready-to-use analyses for assessments, legal services, beneficiaries, and awareness data, plus a Custom Builder for filtered charts and pivot tables without coding. |
| Data Explorer | Enables controlled search, filtering, sorting, and export of loaded data. |
| Beneficiary Cases | Brings together a beneficiary's registration, assessments, services, follow-up, and fees for authorized review. |

## Data protection and proposed safeguards

The application operates locally. Each user would download only the CSV files they are already authorized to access in Knack and load them into the application on an approved Mission device. The platform does not transfer loaded case data to Google AI or any external AI provider for processing. Access to the source data, secure-device requirements, handling of exports, and any sharing of outputs would remain governed by existing INTERSOS data-protection, information-management, and access-control procedures.

No live protection data was used during development. Google AI was used only as a development aid with non-sensitive test data. The production application does not use Google AI, or any other AI service, to process live case data.

This approach is intended to support the principles in SOP #52, including data minimization, transparency, human oversight, and staff accountability. In particular, the application is designed as a staff-controlled review and reporting tool, not as a substitute for professional judgement or established protection procedures.
